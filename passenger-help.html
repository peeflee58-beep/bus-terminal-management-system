STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Use ATC

01

Passenger Registration

  1. Click Register on the home page.
  2. Enter your Full Name.
  3. Enter your Username.
  4. Enter your Email Address.
  5. Create a secure Password.
  6. Click Register.
  7. Login using your Username and Password.
02

How to Book a Ticket

  1. Login to your account.
  2. Open Available Schedules.
  3. Select your preferred State.
  4. Select the Bus.
  5. Select the Route.
  6. Choose an available Seat.
  7. Enter passenger details if required.
  8. Click Book Ticket.
  9. Print or email your ticket after a successful booking.
COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Contact the terminal before the departure time for assistance with your booking.

Can I choose my seat?

Yes. Available seats are displayed during the booking process, allowing you to select an available seat.

How do I receive my ticket?

After a successful booking, you can print your ticket or receive it through email.

What happens if I miss my bus?

Contact the terminal immediately. Rescheduling depends on availability and terminal policy.

How can I contact customer support?

Use the Contact page or visit the terminal's customer service desk for assistance.

What if I need more assistance?

Contact the terminal support team for assistance with bookings, schedules or other passenger services.
