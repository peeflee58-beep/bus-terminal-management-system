ABOUT THE PLATFORM

System Description

The Bus Terminal Management System is a web-based application developed to automate the daily activities of a bus terminal. It simplifies bus management, route planning, scheduling, passenger booking, ticket generation, reporting and revenue monitoring.

The system is designed to improve operational efficiency, minimize manual errors and provide a better experience for both terminal staff and passengers through centralized digital management.
