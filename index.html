ABOUT BTMS

One Platform for Better Operations.

The Bus Terminal Management System (BTMS) is a digital platform designed to simplify and improve the management of modern bus terminal operations.

The system brings essential activities such as bus management, route planning, scheduling, ticket booking, passenger management and reporting into one centralized environment.
