COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

01

How do I add a new bus?

Go to Bus Management, fill in the required bus details and click Add Bus to register the bus in the system.

02

How do I create a route?

Open Route Management, enter the required route information and save the route to make it available for scheduling.

03

How do I create a schedule?

Navigate to Schedule Management, select the appropriate route and bus, enter the departure date and time, then save the schedule.

04

How do passengers book tickets?

Passengers select their state, choose an available bus and route, provide their required details and complete the booking process.

05

How do I restore a backup?

Open Backup & Restore, select the appropriate backup file and click Restore to recover the saved system data.
